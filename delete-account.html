Last Wave — Delete your account

Nakul Creations · app com.lastwave.game

Signing in to Last Wave with Google is optional. If you did sign in, you can delete the account and all data attached to it at any time.

In the game (immediate)

  • Open Last Wave and tap the GOOGLE: your name button on the home screen.
  • Tap DELETE ACCOUNT, then tap TAP AGAIN TO DELETE to confirm.
  • The Firebase account (your Google user ID, display name and email address) is deleted at once and the game signs you out.

Without the game installed

Email voranakul1@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Last Wave account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted, and what is kept

  • Deleted: the account and everything stored with it (Google user ID, display name, email address, sign-in metadata). No other data is attached to the account.
  • Not affected: your local progress on the phone, which is not linked to the account. Remove it with Settings → RESET PROGRESS or by uninstalling. Advertising data is held by Google under its own policy (see the privacy policy).
