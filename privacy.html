Last Wave — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Last Wave ("the game") is made by Nakul Creations ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why. It applies to the Android app com.lastwave.game distributed on Google Play. The same text is published at https://naaaakuu.github.io/lastwave-site/privacy.html and linked from the game (Settings → ABOUT → PRIVACY POLICY).

Summary

  • The game works fully offline and without an account.
  • We do not run our own servers.
  • Google services are used for: ads (Google AdMob), anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics) and the online leaderboards (Firebase), both of which you can switch off, and, only if you choose to sign in, your account (Google Sign-In through Firebase Authentication).
  • There is no crash-reporting SDK in the game.

1. Data the game itself stores on your device

Your player name, sound/graphics/vibration settings, your progress (survivors unlocked, runs played, contracts, mastery, records) and an ad-frequency counter are stored in the game's private storage on your phone. Android Auto Backup is disabled, so this data is not copied to your Google account. Settings → RESET PROGRESS erases your progress; uninstalling the game removes everything it stored.

2. Local multiplayer (LAN)

When you host or join a room, the game talks directly to the other phones on the same Wi-Fi network (your player name, chosen survivor, room name and match data). Nothing about a LAN match is sent over the internet and nothing is sent to us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows two kinds of ads: optional rewarded ads that only play when you tap a button to watch one (to unlock the Brawler), and occasional full-screen ads between solo runs (never in your first three finished solo runs, then at most once every third run, and only after you tap PLAY AGAIN / TRY AGAIN). Ads are never shown during gameplay or in local multiplayer.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). When an ad is requested, the SDK collects and shares with Google information from your device, which may include:

  • the advertising ID and app set ID,
  • IP address, from which an approximate (coarse) location is derived,
  • device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language),
  • ad interactions (views, clicks, video completion) and diagnostic data.

Google uses this to deliver, measure and, where you have allowed it, personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. Details: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy. Ad content in the game is capped at Google's "T" (teen) rating.

Your choices

  • Where the law requires consent (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form

is shown before any ad is requested. You can consent, refuse or manage your options, and you can change your choice later from Settings → AD PRIVACY.

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Android

settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

  • You never have to watch a rewarded ad, and the game is fully playable without ads or an

internet connection.

4. Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics)

To learn where players get stuck and what is worth improving, the game sends anonymous usage statistics to Google Analytics (the same Firebase project, last-wave-fa39f).

What is sent: a random ID the game makes on your phone the first time it runs (it is not your Google account, name, email or advertising ID, and is not linked to any of them), and events such as: opening the game, starting and finishing a run (map, difficulty, survivor, modifier, how far you got, time, kills, experience earned), waves cleared, what put your survivor down, upgrade cards picked, and whether a full-screen ad was shown. Also the game version, graphics setting, how many survivors you have unlocked, and basic device information (model, Android version, language). Google receives your IP address with each request and uses it to derive an approximate location (country level); we do not receive or store IP addresses.

What is not sent: your name, your email, anything you type, your contacts, your precise location, or anything about a LAN match beyond the events above.

Your choices. Settings → SHARE USAGE DATA switches it off; anything not yet sent is discarded. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, where consent is required, the game does not send usage statistics at all. Because the ID is random and not linked to you, we cannot tell which data is yours; clearing the game's data or reinstalling gives the game a new ID.

Google processes this data for us under its terms: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Online leaderboards (Firebase)

After a DAILY RUN or an ENDLESS run, the game posts your best result to a public leaderboard that other players see in the game. To do this it signs in to Firebase Authentication anonymously (no account, name or email: only a random user ID made for this install) and stores your row in our Firebase Realtime Database (project last-wave-fa39f, run by Google, data stored in Singapore).

What a row holds: that random user ID, a nickname the game makes from it (for example GRIM-WOLF-482; you never type a name), your score (Daily Run) or the wave you reached and your kills (Endless), the run's length (Daily Run), the survivor you played, and when it was posted. Only a new best is posted. These rows are public: anyone playing the game can see the nickname, survivor and score. Google receives your IP address with each request, as with any connection. The ID is not linked to your Google account, name, email, advertising ID or usage statistics.

Your choices. Settings → SHARE USAGE DATA off also stops all posting (the boards can still be viewed). In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game does not post. Clearing the game's data or reinstalling gives the game a new ID. To have your rows removed, email us (below) with the nickname the board shows next to "(YOU)"; we delete them within 30 days.

6. Optional Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication)

Signing in is optional and unlocks nothing. If you tap SIGN IN WITH GOOGLE, Google's account picker gives the game a sign-in token, which the game sends to Firebase Authentication (a Google service; our project is last-wave-fa39f) to create or sign in to an account. The account holds your Google user ID, display name and email address, and Firebase keeps standard authentication metadata (sign-in times, IP address). The game also keeps your name, email and a session token on your device so you stay signed in.

We use this data only to identify your account. We do not use it for advertising, we do not share it with anyone other than Google (which processes it for us), and we do not combine it with your gameplay data. Firebase's data handling is described at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Deleting your account. In the game: menu → your account button → DELETE ACCOUNT (tap twice). This deletes the Firebase account and its data immediately and signs you out on the device. If you no longer have the game installed, follow https://naaaakuu.github.io/lastwave-site/delete-account.html or email us (below) with the address you signed in with; we delete the account within 30 days. Nothing else is kept.

7. Data retention

  • On-device data: until you reset progress or uninstall.
  • Account data (sign-in only): until you delete the account.
  • AdMob data: retained by Google under its own policy; we never receive it.
  • Usage statistics: kept in Google Analytics for up to 14 months.
  • Leaderboard rows: kept until we remove them or you ask us to delete yours.

8. Security

All connections to Google services, including usage statistics and the leaderboards, use HTTPS. LAN play stays on your local network.

9. Children

The game is rated for teenagers and adults, is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted at the same address with a new date.

11. Contact

Feedback and sharing. Settings → SEND FEEDBACK opens your own email app with a message to us, pre-filled with the game version and your device model; nothing is sent unless you send it. If you email us we receive your email address and what you write, use them only to reply and to improve the game, and delete them on request. Settings → SHARE GAME opens Android’s share sheet with a link to the game, and RATE GAME opens its Google Play page; the game does not see what you share or with whom.
